NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is my consultation confidential? +

Conversations with qualified counselors and support services are generally handled confidentially, subject to applicable safety and legal requirements.

Is university counseling free? +

If your institution provides a free Student Care or Counseling Center, students can access those services without an additional consultation fee.

When should I seek professional help? +

You can reach out whenever you feel overwhelmed, unable to cope, or simply want someone qualified to talk to. You do not need to wait until things become a crisis.
